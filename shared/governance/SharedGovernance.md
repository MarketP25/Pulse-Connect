# Shared Governance in PULSCO

## Overview

PULSCO's shared governance model is a foundational aspect of its "governance-first" design philosophy. It establishes a multi-layered, distributed framework for policy enforcement, auditability, security, and compliance across all planetary-scale subsystems. This ensures that every action, decision, and data flow adheres to predefined rules, maintains data integrity, and provides immutable records for transparency and accountability.

## Core Principles

The shared governance architecture is built upon several key principles:

- **Governance-First**: Policies and compliance are integrated into the system's design from inception, not as afterthoughts.
- **Zero-Trust Architecture**: Every request and interaction, regardless of origin, is continuously validated against defined policies.
- **Immutability**: All critical actions and decisions generate tamper-evident, hash-chained audit logs, preventing retrospective alteration.
- **Transparency**: Audit trails, policy evaluations, and decision contexts are available for inspection (where appropriate), fostering trust.
- **Policy-Driven**: Operations are driven by explicit, configurable policies, enabling dynamic adaptation and control.
- **Distributed Enforcement**: Governance is enforced at the edge, at the core intelligence layer, and within individual subsystems.

## Key Components and Their Roles

Shared governance in PULSCO is orchestrated through the interplay of several core components:

### 1. MARP Governance Core (`services/marp-governance-core`)

- **Role**: The primary engine for policy evaluation, enforcement, and audit trail generation. It acts as the central authority for defining and interpreting governance rules.
- **Functions**:
  - **Policy Evaluation & Enforcement**: Interprets and applies Market Arbitration/Review Policies (MARP) to subsystem calls and decisions.
  - **Audit Trail Generation**: Creates immutable, hash-chained audit logs for all governed actions, including policy versions and decision contexts.
  - **Consent Management**: Manages and enforces user consent for data sharing and processing, ensuring immutability of consent.
  - **Compliance Verification**: Provides mechanisms to verify adherence to regulatory and internal compliance standards.
  - **Council Voting / Override Rules**: Supports advanced governance workflows, potentially involving human oversight or dynamic policy adjustments.

### 2. Edge Gateway (`services/marp-firewall-gateway`)

- **Role**: The global execution surface and firewall that protects subsystem calls and decisions, acting as the first line of governance enforcement.
- **Functions**:
  - **Policy Enforcement**: Integrates with MARP to enforce policies at the entry point of the platform, protecting subsystems.
  - **Security Enforcement**: Validates user permissions, tokens, and context for every request.
  - **Adapter Registry**: Manages a registry of subsystem adapters, with policy-scoped capabilities and health signaling.
  - **Audit Sink**: Collects audit events generated by MARP and other components, contributing to the central audit chain.
  - **Traffic Management**: Routes requests and aggregates data from multiple subsystems in a governed manner.

### 3. Central Super Intelligence (CSI) (`packages/csi`)

- **Role**: Provides event-driven advisory intelligence, analysis, and recommendations, and drives governance workflows.
- **Functions**:
  - **Governance Workflows**: Drives automated and semi-automated governance processes based on real-time analysis.
  - **Policy Directives**: Can issue directives based on insights, influencing or overriding MARP policies under specific conditions (with audit trails).
  - **Audit Analysis**: Ingests and analyzes audit events, providing insights into compliance posture and potential risks.
  - **Explainability Hooks**: Provides context and reasoning for decisions made, enhancing transparency.

### 4. Shared Security and Audit Primitives (`shared/lib`)

- **Role**: Provides fundamental building blocks for security and audit capabilities used across the platform.
- **Functions**:
  - **PC365 Dual Control**: Enforces a "dual control" mechanism for sensitive operations, requiring multiple approvals.
  - **Hash Chaining**: Ensures the tamper-evidence of audit logs, critical for maintaining immutable records.
  - **Consent/Purpose Flags**: Mechanisms to enforce and capture consent and data usage purposes at the point of action.

## Governance Flows and Mechanisms

### Policy Enforcement Flow

1. **User Request**: Initiates from PULSCO Portal, targeting a specific subsystem.
2. **Edge Gateway Intercept**: Edge Gateway receives the request and validates initial security parameters (e.g., tokens).
3. **MARP Policy Check**: Edge Gateway forwards relevant context to MARP Governance. MARP evaluates policies (e.g., access, data usage, compliance) against the request and user context.
4. **Decision & Audit**: MARP makes a policy decision (allow/deny/modify) and generates immutable audit events, recording the policy applied and the decision.
5. **Subsystem Execution**: If allowed, the Edge routes the request to the appropriate PULSCO subsystem. Subsystems also interact with MARP to ensure ongoing policy compliance during execution (e.g., before performing a critical action).
6. **Results & Compliance Status**: Results are returned to the Portal, potentially with compliance status metadata.

### Audit & Accountability

- Every critical action, decision, and policy evaluation within the Edge, MARP, and core subsystems generates append-only, hash-chained audit logs.
- These logs include the context of the action, the policy version applied, and consent flags, ensuring full traceability and non-repudiation.
- CSI continuously monitors and analyzes these audit streams for anomalies or compliance breaches.

### Consent Management

- User consent is managed centrally by MARP, providing an immutable record of granted permissions.
- Subsystems enforce consent flags at call sites (e.g., for marketing communications, data sharing with third parties) and capture these in audit records.

### Risk Management & Compliance

- **Security Hardening**: Implemented across all infrastructure, including network security, secret management, and firewall rules.
- **Performance Monitoring**: Ensures that governance overhead does not compromise system performance, with alerts configured for deviations.
- **Data Integrity**: Comprehensive backup, rollback, and data validation procedures prevent data loss or corruption.
- **Incident Response**: Clearly defined procedures for identifying, responding to, and mitigating incidents, with immediate communication protocols.
- **Rollback Procedures**: Automated and manual rollback plans, including data rollback strategies, are in place to address deployment or functional failures.

### Decision Explainability

- PULSCO aims to provide explainability hooks within its decision and policy domains. This means that for complex decisions made by AI (CSI) or policy engines (MARP), the reasoning and underlying policies can be surfaced.

## Operational Aspects

- **Monitoring**: Extensive monitoring and observability, including APM, centralized logging, and dashboard setup, track governance metrics and system health.
- **Team Training**: Operations and support teams are trained on governance procedures, incident response, and the overall operational model.
- **Runbooks**: Detailed runbooks, such as for the Edge Gateway and SEO Discovery Engine, incorporate governance considerations like internal service tokens and CSI governance decisions.

## Benefits of Shared Governance

- **Enhanced Security**: A multi-layered defense ensures comprehensive protection against unauthorized access and malicious activities.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Simplifies adherence to global data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) through robust consent management and audit trails.
- **Increased Trust**: Transparency and immutability build confidence among users and stakeholders regarding data handling and system operations.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Automated policy enforcement reduces manual oversight, while clear audit trails streamline debugging and forensics.
- **Scalability**: The distributed nature of governance components allows the system to scale globally without compromising control or compliance.
- **Adaptability**: Policy-driven architecture allows for agile adaptation to evolving business needs or regulatory landscapes.

This shared governance framework is central to PULSCO's mission of building a secure, transparent, and intelligent planetary-scale platform.
