## 3. Core Definitions

*   **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
*   **Processing**: Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, analysis, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, or erasure.
*   **CSI (Central Super Intelligence)**: Pulsco’s proprietary, centralized AI learning layer. The CSI processes platform-wide data to enhance service intelligence, personalization, risk detection, and operational efficiency across the entire ecosystem.
*   **MARP (Multi-layered Autonomous Response & Protection)**: Pulsco’s comprehensive governance, security, and arbitration framework that enforces policies and manages risk across all interconnected services.

## 4. Data We Collect: An Ecosystem-Wide Approach

We collect data across the entire Pulsco ecosystem to provide a seamless, intelligent, and secure experience. The categories of data we collect are interconnected and processed by our CSI.

**4.1. Identity & Account Data**
*   Full name, username, email, phone number, and hashed passwords.
*   **Know-Your-Customer (KYC) & Verification Data**: Government-issued ID numbers, copies of identification documents, proof of address, and biometric data (e.g., facial geometry from a selfie) for identity verification and fraud prevention, as required by law and our internal risk policies.
*   Profile information, such as photos, user biography, and language preferences.

**4.2. Transactional & Financial Data**
*   **Marketplace & E-commerce Data**: Purchase and sale history, order details, cart contents, products viewed, seller/buyer ratings, and reviews.
*   **Wallet & Billing Data**: Pulsco Wallet balance, transaction history, stored value, payment and payout instructions, bank account details (tokenized where possible), and billing information.
*   **Payment Data**: Tokenized payment card information, transaction identifiers, and fraud risk scores. We do not store full payment card numbers on our core systems.

**4.3. Geolocation & Proximity Data**
*   **Precise Geolocation Data**: Real-time location from your device's GPS, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth signals (with your consent) to power proximity services, delivery tracking, and location-based matchmaking.
*   **Approximate Location**: Location derived from your IP address or voluntarily provided (e.g., city).
*   **Location History**: Historical location data used by the CSI to optimize logistics, understand traffic patterns, and improve the accuracy of our geocoding engine.

**4.4. Communication & Content Data**
*   Content of messages, voice calls, and video communications within our chat systems.
*   Metadata of communications (time, duration, participants).
*   Content you create, upload, or share, such as business listings, reviews, and support tickets.

**4.5. Behavioral & Technical Data**
*   **Usage Data**: Features used, actions taken, time spent, and navigation paths within the Services. This data is a primary input for the CSI.
*   **Device & Network Data**: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, mobile network information, and unique device identifiers.
*   **Cookie & Tracking Data**: Data from cookies, pixels, and similar technologies used for analytics, personalization, and advertising.

**4.6. Inferred & Generated Data**
*   **CSI-Generated Insights**: The CSI analyzes data from all the above categories to generate new data points, such as user interest profiles, fraud risk scores, predicted behavior, and social graph connections. These insights are core to the ecosystem's intelligence.
*   **Governance & Arbitration Data**: Records of policy violations, dispute evidence, arbitration proceedings, and enforcement actions taken under the MARP framework.

## 6. AI, Automated Decision-Making, and the CSI

Pulsco is an intelligence-driven ecosystem. The CSI makes automated decisions and generates insights that are integral to the Services.

*   **Automated Systems**: Our services rely on automated systems for content moderation, fraud detection, transaction risk analysis, and matchmaking. For example, a transaction may be automatically flagged or blocked if it meets high-risk criteria.
*   **CSI Learning**: The CSI continuously learns from every interaction on the platform—a purchase, a search query, a message, a delivery route. This platform-wide learning allows it to build sophisticated models of user behavior, market trends, and logistical efficiencies.
*   **Your Rights**: Where an automated decision significantly or legally affects you (e.g., account suspension, wallet asset freeze), you have the right to request human review, express your point of view, and contest the decision. Such escalations are handled via the MARP arbitration framework.

## 7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. We share data only as necessary to operate the ecosystem:

*   **Within the Pulsco Ecosystem**: Data is shared across our interconnected services to ensure a unified experience. For example, your marketplace transaction history may inform the recommendations you see in the business directory.
*   **With Other Users**: As necessary to facilitate interactions, such as sharing your delivery address with a seller or your profile with a matched user.
*   **With Service Providers**: With vendors and partners who provide services on our behalf, such as payment processing, cloud hosting (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud), KYC verification, and customer support. They are contractually bound to protect your data.
*   **For Legal Reasons**: To comply with Kenyan and international laws, subpoenas, or court orders. We may also share data to enforce our policies, protect our rights, or ensure the safety of our users.
*   **In Corporate Transactions**: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred, subject to the new entity upholding the promises in this policy.

## 8. Security and the MARP Framework

We have engineered the MARP framework as a core security layer. We implement investor-grade technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

*   **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
*   **Access Control**: Strict, role-based access controls and a "least privilege" principle are enforced. Founder-level and other high-privilege access require multi-factor authentication and are subject to real-time auditing.
*   **Wallet Security**: The Pulsco Wallet infrastructure is segregated and protected with additional security layers, including controls over asset freezes and payouts.
*   **Data Retention**: We retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations (e.g., financial records), and to resolve disputes.
*   **International Data Transfers**: As a global platform, we transfer data internationally. We ensure such transfers are legal and secure by using mechanisms like the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and by vetting the security practices of our partners.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

In line with GDPR and CCPA principles, you have the following rights over your data:

*   **Right to Access**: You can request a copy of the Personal Data we hold about you.
*   **Right to Rectification**: You can correct inaccurate data.
*   **Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten')**: You can request that we delete your data, subject to our legal and contractual retention needs.
*   **Right to Restrict Processing**: You can ask us to limit the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
*   **Right to Data Portability**: You can request your data in a machine-readable format.
*   **Right to Object**: You can object to our processing of your data for reasons related to your particular situation, especially where we rely on Legitimate Interest.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at **privacy@pulsco.com**. We will respond to verifiable requests in accordance with applicable law.
