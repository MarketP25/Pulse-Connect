# Pulsco Ecosystem - Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Effective Date: February 28, 2026**

## Preamble: Maintaining a Trusted Global Ecosystem

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a binding component of the Pulsco Terms of Service. Its purpose is to protect the integrity, security, and fairness of the entire Pulsco ecosystem—a complex infrastructure combining commerce, geolocation, AI, communications, and financial services.

Adherence to this AUP is mandatory for all users. This policy is not exhaustive. We reserve the right to take enforcement action against any activity that we deem harmful, fraudulent, or in violation of the spirit of the Pulsco platform, even if not explicitly listed here.

Violations of this AUP will be adjudicated under the **Platform Governance & Enforcement Charter (MARP)** and may result in immediate and severe penalties, including account termination and the freezing of all assets held in your Pulsco Wallet.

---

### SECTION 1: ILLEGAL, HARMFUL, AND HATEFUL ACTIVITIES

You may not use the Pulsco Services to engage in, promote, or facilitate any activity that is illegal in the jurisdictions where we operate. This includes, but is not limited to:

*   **Trafficking & Prohibited Goods**: The sale, exchange, or facilitation of any illegal goods or services, including narcotics, weapons, counterfeit items, and any product or service restricted by applicable law.
*   **Exploitation**: Content or activities that exploit, harm, or endanger children or any other individual.
*   **Hate Speech & Harassment**: Content or behavior that promotes violence, incites hatred, or harasses individuals or groups based on race, ethnic origin, religion, disability, age, nationality, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, or any other characteristic associated with systemic discrimination or marginalization.
*   **Intellectual Property Infringement**: Violating the copyrights, trademarks, patents, or trade secrets of others.

### SECTION 2: PLATFORM & SYSTEM INTEGRITY

The technical integrity of our planetary-scale infrastructure is paramount. Any attempt to compromise it will be met with maximum enforcement. You are strictly prohibited from:

*   **Unauthorized Access & Reverse Engineering**: Accessing non-public areas of the Services, our systems, or the technical delivery systems of our providers. You may not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of any system or network or attempt to reverse-engineer our software, AI models (including the CSI), or proprietary protocols, except where permitted by law.
*   **Automated Attacks & Scraping**: Launching Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks, engaging in high-volume, unauthorized data scraping, or otherwise placing an unreasonable load on our infrastructure.
*   **Malicious Software**: Distributing viruses, worms, malware, or any other code intended to disrupt, damage, or limit the functioning of any software, hardware, or telecommunications equipment.
*   **Circumventing Security**: Bypassing or attempting to bypass any security measure, authentication system, rate limit, or governance control, including the MARP framework itself.

### SECTION 3: ECONOMIC & MARKETPLACE INTEGRITY

The fairness and reliability of our marketplace, directory, and financial systems are essential. You are strictly prohibited from:

*   **Financial Fraud**: Engaging in any form of fraud, including credit card fraud, payment fraud, or chargeback abuse.
*   **Money Laundering**: Using the Pulsco Wallet or any part of the Services to facilitate money laundering or terrorist financing.
*   **Marketplace Manipulation**: Creating fake listings, posting fraudulent reviews, manipulating ratings or search rankings, or engaging in any form of collusion to distort the marketplace.
*   **Spam**: Using our automated marketing or communication tools to send unsolicited and unauthorized commercial messages.

### SECTION 4: AI & DATA INTEGRITY

Our Central Super Intelligence (CSI) and other AI systems rely on high-quality, trusted data. Corrupting these systems is a serious offense. You are strictly prohibited from:

*   **AI Abuse**: Using our AI tools to generate harmful, illegal, or deceptive content. This includes attempting to bypass safety filters or prompting the AI to produce content that violates any other section of this AUP.
*   **Data Poisoning & Model Manipulation**: Intentionally feeding our systems false, biased, or malicious data in an attempt to manipulate the CSI's learning process, skew matchmaking results, or degrade service performance.
*   **Geolocation Spoofing**: Falsifying your device's GPS, IP address, or other location signals to mislead our proximity engine, commit fraud, or manipulate location-based services.

### SECTION 5: USER & CONTENT INTEGRITY

Trust between users is foundational. Deception and misrepresentation erode the ecosystem. You are strictly prohibited from:

*   **Impersonation & Identity Misuse**: Impersonating another person or entity, creating synthetic (fake) identities, or engaging in phishing or social engineering to acquire the credentials or personal information of other users.
*   **Deceptive Content**: Posting or transmitting content that is false, misleading, or deceptive.
*   **Communication Abuse**: Using our real-time communication tools to harass, threaten, or spam other users.

### SECTION 6: ENFORCEMENT

As outlined in our Terms of Service, we will enforce this AUP at our sole discretion. Enforcement actions are immediate and may include:

*   Content removal and listing takedowns.
*   Temporary or permanent feature restrictions.
*   **Immediate wallet holds or the freezing of all financial assets.**
*   Account suspension or permanent termination without notice.
*   Reporting to law enforcement authorities where appropriate.

### SECTION 7: REPORTING VIOLATIONS

If you become aware of any violation of this AUP, you are obligated to report it to our governance team immediately. Retaliation against any user for reporting a violation is itself a serious violation of this policy.

**To report a violation, please contact: dto@pulsco.com**
