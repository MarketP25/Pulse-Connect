# The Pulsco Platform Governance & Enforcement Charter

**Effective Date: February 28, 2026**

## PREAMBLE: THE RULE OF LAW IN A DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM

This Charter establishes the framework for governance, enforcement, and dispute resolution for the entire Pulsco ecosystem. It is the supreme document that gives authority to the enforcement actions detailed in the Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and all other platform policies. Its purpose is to ensure the Pulsco ecosystem operates on the principles of security, fairness, transparency, and accountability.

All users, by using the Services, submit to the jurisdiction of this Charter and the governance bodies it establishes.

---

### ARTICLE I: THE MARP FRAMEWORK

1.  **Definition**: The **MARP (Multi-layered Autonomous Response & Protection)** framework is Pulsco's proprietary governance system. It is a synthesis of automated AI-driven controls and structured human oversight designed to manage a planetary-scale digital ecosystem.
2.  **Guiding Principles**: MARP operates according to four core principles:
    *   **Security**: To protect the integrity of the platform, its users, and its assets from internal and external threats.
    *   **Fairness**: To apply platform policies consistently and provide a clear process for appeal and redress.
    *   **Transparency**: To be open about the rules and the mechanisms of their enforcement, as exemplified by this Charter.
    *   **Enforcement**: To act decisively to remove bad actors and remedy harm within the ecosystem.

### ARTICLE II: THE GOVERNANCE HIERARCHY

MARP establishes a clear escalation hierarchy for adjudication and dispute resolution.

1.  **Tier 1: Automated Governance (CSI)**
    *   The Central Super Intelligence (CSI) serves as the first line of defense, automatically enforcing clear-cut violations of the Acceptable Use Policy (e.g., spam, prohibited content, malicious file uploads) and identifying high-risk transactions.
    *   Actions at this tier are immediate and may include content removal or temporary, automated holds on transactions.

2.  **Tier 2: Internal Review & Support Teams**
    *   This is the primary level for human review. These teams handle initial user appeals against automated actions, respond to support tickets, and investigate routine policy violations reported by the community.
    *   They have the authority to reverse automated decisions, issue formal warnings, and apply minor sanctions.

3.  **Tier 3: The Pulsco Arbitration Council**
    *   **Mandate**: The Council is a formal, internal arbitral body established to adjudicate significant disputes that are not resolved at Tier 2. Its jurisdiction includes, but is not limited to, appeals concerning permanent account termination, large-scale asset freezes, and complex marketplace disputes.
    *   **Composition**: The Council is composed of senior Pulsco employees with specialized training in platform policy, dispute resolution, and regional law.
    *   **Finality of Decisions**: Decisions rendered by the Arbitration Council are final and binding on all parties, subject only to the oversight of the Founders.

4.  **Tier 4: Founder-Level Oversight**
    *   **Authority**: Ultimate authority over the ecosystem rests with the Founders of Pulsco or their designated successors.
    *   **Jurisdiction**: Founder-level review is reserved for the most critical ecosystem-level decisions, including amendments to this Charter, rulings that have a significant impact on the platform's strategic direction, and resolving any conflicts within the governance structure itself.

### ARTICLE III: ENUMERATED ENFORCEMENT POWERS

The governance bodies established by this Charter have the authority to levy the following sanctions, with or without prior notice, depending on the severity of the violation:

1.  **Content Moderation**: Removal or restricted visibility of any listing, post, or user-generated content that violates platform policies.
2.  **Feature Limitation**: Restricting access to certain features, such as the ability to list new items, use the marketing engine, or communicate with other users.
3.  **Account Suspension**: A temporary revocation of all account privileges.
4.  **Account Termination**: The permanent and irrevocable closure of a user's account and barring from future use of the Services.
5.  **Wallet Enforcement**:
    *   **Holds**: A temporary hold on specific transaction funds to allow for investigation.
    *   **Reserves**: A requirement for a Seller to maintain a minimum balance in their wallet to cover potential chargebacks or disputes.
    *   **Asset Freeze**: The complete freezing of all funds, balances, and stored value within a Pulsco Wallet, pending the outcome of a Tier 3 or Tier 4 investigation. This power is used in cases of suspected severe fraud, money laundering, or other serious crimes.

### ARTICLE IV: THE ARBITRATION PROTOCOL

The process for escalating a dispute to the Pulsco Arbitration Council (Tier 3) is as follows:

1.  **Prerequisite**: A user must have first attempted to resolve the issue through the Internal Review & Support Teams (Tier 2) and received a formal decision.
2.  **Initiation**: An appeal may be initiated within 30 days of a Tier 2 decision by filing a formal "Request for Arbitration" through the specified channel in the user's account portal.
3.  **Evidence Submission**: Both the user and the Pulsco governance team will be provided a window to submit all relevant evidence, including logs, communications, and transaction records.
4.  **Adjudication**: The Arbitration Council will review the evidence and render a written decision within a specified timeframe. The decision is final.

### ARTICLE V: EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

In the event of a severe, system-wide threat to the Pulsco ecosystem (such as a major security breach, a coordinated economic attack, or a critical legal/regulatory demand), Founder-Level Authority may invoke the Emergency Protocol. This protocol grants the authority to take immediate, sweeping actions necessary to protect the platform, including but not limited to:

*   Temporarily disabling major features or services.
*   Implementing mass freezes on transactions or wallet payouts.
*   Blocking access from certain regions or IP ranges.

#### Implementation Note (Global Control Plane)

The Emergency Protocol is technically enforced through the SuperAdmin control surface and backend governance services, with CSI-linked emergency intelligence and cross-system policy propagation. Emergency activation/deactivation events are founder-gated, audited, and propagated to enforcement endpoints that can block sensitive operations (including transaction and payout paths) during active emergency states.

### ARTICLE VI: AMENDMENTS

This Charter may only be amended by a formal decree from the Founder-Level Authority (Tier 4). All users will be notified of any material changes to this Charter.
