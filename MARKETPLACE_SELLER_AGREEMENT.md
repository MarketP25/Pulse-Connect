Your use of the marketplace is subject to the following financial terms:

*   **Platform Commission**: In consideration for your use of the platform, Pulsco will charge a **Platform Commission** on all successful transactions. Unless otherwise specified in your Seller dashboard, this commission is calculated as a percentage of the total transaction value (including the item price, shipping, and other charges).
