# Pulsco Ecosystem - AI & Automation Disclosure

**Effective Date: February 28, 2026**

## 1. Our Commitment to Transparency

Pulsco is an intelligence-driven ecosystem. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is not just a feature; it is the core of our platform, designed to create a more efficient, personalized, and secure experience for everyone. This document discloses how we use AI, including our proprietary **Central Super Intelligence (CSI)**, and how it impacts your use of the Services. Its purpose is to foster transparency and trust.

This disclosure is part of our legal framework and should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 2. The Central Super Intelligence (CSI)

The CSI is Pulsco's proprietary, centralized AI learning layer. Think of it as the brain of the ecosystem. It is designed to learn from the collective activity across all interconnected services—from marketplace transactions to geolocation signals. This platform-wide learning allows the CSI to build sophisticated models that improve every aspect of the Services in real-time.

## 3. How AI Powers the Pulsco Ecosystem

We use AI and automation in numerous ways. Below are some key examples of how these systems operate across our platform's layers.

*   **Marketplace & Discovery**:
    *   **Personalization**: The CSI analyzes your browsing history, purchases, and searches to power the recommendations, search results, and content you see.
    *   **Dynamic Ranking**: Products and services are ranked based on relevance, quality, seller reputation, and your inferred interests.

*   **Geolocation & Proximity Engine**:
    *   **Route & ETA Optimization**: For services involving delivery, our AI calculates the most efficient routes and predicts arrival times by analyzing real-time traffic and historical data.
    *   **Proximity Discovery**: The AI helps you discover relevant businesses, services, or events near your current location.

*   **Matchmaking Systems**:
    *   Our AI-driven matchmaking engine connects users (e.g., for services, social features, or commerce) based on complex, learned patterns of compatibility, preferences, and behavior, going far beyond simple filters.

*   **Governance, Security, and Risk (MARP Framework)**:
    *   **Fraud Detection**: The CSI is our primary defense against fraud. It analyzes thousands of signals in real-time to score the risk of transactions, logins, and new account creations.
    *   **Policy Enforcement**: Our AI systems automatically scan for content and behavior that violates our Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), flagging it for removal or review.

*   **Automated Marketing & Communications**:
    *   **Marketing Engine**: For sellers, our tools can automate marketing campaigns, optimizing them based on performance data and user engagement.
    *   **Real-time Translation**: Our communication tools use AI to provide real-time translation, breaking down language barriers across our global user base.

## 4. Automated Decision-Making and Your Rights

Some decisions within the Pulsco ecosystem are made automatically, without human intervention. We are transparent about this and provide you with recourse.

*   **Types of Automated Decisions**: Significant automated decisions can include:
    *   Blocking a high-risk payment or transaction.
    *   Placing a temporary hold or freeze on wallet assets.
    *   Suspending an account for a severe AUP violation.
    *   Denying an application to become a seller based on risk criteria.

*   **Your Right to Human Review**: As stated in our Terms of Service, where an automated decision produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you, **you have the right to request human intervention.** You can contest the decision, express your point of view, and have the case reviewed by a member of our governance team under the MARP framework.

## 5. The Learning Process and Our Approach to Bias

*   **Learning from Ecosystem Activity**: The CSI's intelligence is derived from its ability to process vast amounts of data generated by user activity on the platform. To protect privacy, this data is pseudonymized or aggregated wherever possible.
*   **Commitment to Fairness**: We recognize that all AI systems have the potential for bias. We are committed to an ongoing process of identifying and mitigating bias in our models to ensure fair and equitable outcomes for all our users. This is a core tenet of our governance philosophy.

## 6. Limitations and Your Responsibility

*   **AI Outputs are "AS IS"**: AI-generated outputs, including recommendations, translations, and insights, are provided on an "as is" basis. They are powerful tools, but they are not infallible. We make no guarantees as to their accuracy, completeness, or suitability for any particular purpose.
*   **Your Responsibility**: You are ultimately responsible for your decisions and actions. Before relying on any critical information provided by our AI systems (e.g., for business or financial decisions), you should independently verify it. As outlined in our Terms of Service, Pulsco is not liable for any outcomes based on your reliance on AI outputs.
